How to Use RentWise

1. Setup Household: Add all roommates and their monthly incomes.
2. Enter Budget: Set your target monthly spending limit for the whole house.
3. Add Expenses: Log rent, utilities, etc., and choose how to split them (Equal, Income-Based, or Custom %).
4. Check Alerts: Check the meters to see if your household or any individual is crossing the safe affordability limit.
